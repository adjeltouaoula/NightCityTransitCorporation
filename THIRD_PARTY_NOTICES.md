# Third-party notices

## Drive a Bus

The passenger-seat selection, mounting-request pattern, and Mahir passenger
cabin entity are adapted from **Drive a Bus 1.2.0** by **tidusMD**, with the
author's explicit permission to reuse and bundle the work provided proper
credit is given on the public mod page.

Original mod: https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/17099

NCTC exposes passenger seats only. It does not include the original garage,
player-driver, or manual bus-driving features.

## Auto Drive Enhanced — experimental branch only

The `experiment/ade-autodrive-route-loop` test build contains a locally patched
copy of `driving_ai.reds` from **Auto Drive Enhanced**. The only NCTC-specific
addition is an explicit-destination entry point used to evaluate ADE's complete
driving command handler without a player-created waypoint. This experiment is
not part of the public release line and must not be redistributed until the
upstream licensing/permission terms have been confirmed.
