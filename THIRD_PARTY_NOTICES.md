# Third-party notices

## Drive a Bus

The passenger-seat selection, mounting-request pattern, and Mahir passenger
cabin entity are adapted from **Drive a Bus 1.2.0** by **tidusMD**, with the
author's explicit permission to reuse and bundle the work provided proper
credit is given on the public mod page.

Original mod: https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/17099

NCTC exposes passenger seats only. It does not include the original garage,
player-driver, or manual bus-driving features.

## Auto Drive Enhanced

The optional standalone traffic-driving compatibility resource and its small
REDscript command bridge are adapted from **Auto Drive Enhanced 0.0.0.20**.
NCTC does not include ADE's player AutoDrive, HUD, settings, cameras, or CET
runtime. The compatibility layer is used only by the NCTC service bus.

Original mod: https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/23157

## Metro Pocket Guide

The NCTC Pocket Guide world-map interaction model is adapted from
**Metro Pocket Guide** by **DJ_Kovrik**, with the author's explicit permission
to reuse and adapt the work for NCTC. The port keeps NCTC-specific class,
field, widget, and runtime names so Metro Pocket Guide and NCTC can remain
installed together.

Original mod: https://www.nexusmods.com/cyberpunk2077/mods/11882
Source: https://github.com/djkovrik/CP77Mods/tree/master/src/Metro%20Pocket%20Guide
